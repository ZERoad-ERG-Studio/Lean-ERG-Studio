Click an interval to select and edit it. With a mouse you can also drag its right edge (length) or its top edge (power). Colours show your power zones.
Target nudge:
Shortcuts: Space start/pause · Up/Down nudge (while riding) · Right skip interval · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo / redo. Weight is used for W/kg only: the standard trainer protocol has no setting for it.
With a power meter paired, ERG follows the meter: about once a second the app fine-tunes the target it sends the trainer until the meter shows the target power. After a big step change (for example 180 to 240 W) it can take up to about 15 seconds to settle, and your power may be off the target for part of that time. No calibration is stored. If the meter signal drops, the app shows the trainer's reading and holds the last adjustment.
